How to play

Each turn you play one card from your hand:

  • TAKE — capture table cards that match your card's value or add up to it.
  • BUILD — set up a value you can capture on a later turn.
  • TRAIL — lay a card down when you can't take.

Score for most cards, most spades, sweeps, aces, the last trick, and the two Kasinos — the 2 of spades and the 10 of diamonds. Most points wins.
